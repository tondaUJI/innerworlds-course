Mental health is a continuum

Everyone moves between green, yellow and red zones over time. No emotional state is permanent, and recognising our own experiences and those of others is a shared responsibility of the whole school community.

GreenFeeling well, coping, connected.
YellowStressed or struggling — time to use coping tools and talk.
RedSignificant distress — seek appropriate support.
